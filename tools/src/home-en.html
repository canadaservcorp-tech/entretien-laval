A garment's last life

Before it's gone, one last useful job.

A T-shirt or towel too worn to resell usually ends up in the trash. We give it one last useful step: becoming a rag that works in a garage, a factory or on a job site, instead of going straight to landfill.

01

Worn

The garment is used for years, then donated or recycled.

02

Recovered

Too worn to resell, it is recovered instead of thrown away.

03

Prepared

Metal and buttons removed, hot-water washed, treated against bedbugs, cut ready to use.

04

Last use

It wipes oil, grease and paint, instead of disposable paper.

Why recycled
