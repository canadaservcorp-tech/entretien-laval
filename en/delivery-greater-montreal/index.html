Greater Montreal

We sort and pack in Laval and deliver across Greater Montreal:

  • Laval: Chomedey, Laval-des-Rapides, Pont-Viau, Vimont, Auteuil, Sainte-Rose, Fabreville, Sainte-Dorothée, Duvernay, Saint-François, Saint-Vincent-de-Paul.
  • Montréal and the island of Montreal.
  • North Shore: Terrebonne, Blainville, Boisbriand, Mirabel, Saint-Eustache, Repentigny.
  • South Shore: Longueuil, Brossard, Boucherville.

Ask us about the terms for your area.

Elsewhere in Québec

We also deliver to the regions, from 1,000 lb per delivery (40 bags, mixed products accepted). The delivery fee depends on distance and is stated in the quote.

How it works

  1. Send your request: product, quantity and delivery city.
  2. We send a quote with the price delivered to your address.
  3. The order is confirmed and paid one week before delivery.
  4. We deliver to the agreed address: office, warehouse, shop or job site.
