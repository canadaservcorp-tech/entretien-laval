Equipment maintenance

Greasing pivot points, oil changes, hydraulic oil, cleaning filters and engines: farm machinery uses a lot of rags, especially before planting and harvest.

Which rag to choose

Colored terry ($32 per 25 lb bag) is the most economical for oil and grease. Order in volume before the season: see wholesale pricing.

Delivery to rural areas

We deliver anywhere in Québec from 1,000 lb (40 bags). Neighbouring farms can combine an order to a single address. See the delivery terms.

Before planting and harvest

Order before the busy seasons: orders are confirmed and paid one week before delivery. Store bags in a dry building, away from fuel and chemicals. Rags soaked with oil or fuel go in a closed metal container, never in a pile in the barn.
