Uses

  • Garages and heavy equipment: oil, grease, coolant.
  • Machining and welding: cutting oil, greasy parts.
  • Farms and trucking: equipment, trucks, hydraulics.
  • Spills: soak up a large volume of liquid fast.

Why this rag

Terry (towel cloth) has loops that hold far more liquid than T-shirt. It stands up to rubbing on rough surfaces and can be reused several times. For light wiping, colored T-shirt is enough.

Quality

Recycled cotton, washed in hot water, treated against bedbugs, with metal, buttons and zippers removed, cut and packed in Laval in 25 lb bags.

Frequently asked questions

Does terry leave lint?

A little more than T-shirt. For surfaces that must stay lint-free, choose T-shirt.

What do I do with oily rags?

Store them in a closed metal container: see our guide oily rags and fire risk.
