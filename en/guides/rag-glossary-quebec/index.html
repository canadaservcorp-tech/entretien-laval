Rag glossary: Québec French and English terms

In Québec, people say guenille, chiffon or linge. In English, rag, wiper or shop rag. Here's what each word means.

Request a quoteinfo@entretienlaval.ca

Words change from one region and trade to the next. This glossary helps you compare offers and order the right product.

Guenille
Québec French for a rag made from old fabric, used for cleaning or wiping. Guenilles are often sold by weight, in bags.
Chiffon d'essuyage (wiping rag)
General term for a piece of fabric used to wipe oil, grease, liquids or dust. Also called a wiper.
Shop rag (chiffon d'atelier)
A rag used in garages and workshops.
Linge
In Québec, "un linge" also means a cleaning cloth or towel, as in "linge à vaisselle" (dish towel).
Torchon
Word used more in France than in Québec for a cleaning rag or dish towel.
Terry (ratine)
Looped towel fabric. Thick and highly absorbent. Québec French calls it ratine.
Jersey (T-shirt)
Knitted cotton fabric from T-shirts. Thin, soft and low-lint.
Low-lint (peu pelucheux)
Leaves few fibers on the surface.
No color transfer (sans transfert de couleur)
White rag that leaves no dye on the wiped surface.
Post-consumer textile
Used clothing and fabric collected after use, then sorted and turned into rags.
Sold by weight
Rags are sold by the pound (lb) rather than by the piece, because piece sizes vary.
Bedbug treatment (traitement anti-punaises)
Treating textiles against bedbugs before packing.
