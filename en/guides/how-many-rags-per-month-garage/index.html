How many shop rags does a garage use per month?

Every garage is different: the number of bays, the type of work and each mechanic's habits change everything. Here's how to measure your real usage in a few weeks.

Why there's no universal number

A general repair shop, a tire centre and a body shop don't wipe the same things. A garage doing lots of oil changes and brake jobs uses more rags than one focused on electronics. Instead of a made-up average, measure your own usage.

ECO Rags: recycled cotton rags in 25 lb bags, coloured $32 · white $42 before tax, delivered in Laval, Montréal and both Shores. Order online · Wholesale (50 bags +)

The 4-step method

  1. Order a starting quantity: 2 to 4 25 lb bags of coloured T-shirt rags are enough to start.
  2. Write the opening date on each bag with a marker.
  3. Write the date it's empty. After two or three bags, you have a reliable average.
  4. Calculate: pounds per week = 25 lb × bags used ÷ number of weeks. Enter that number in the calculator to get your bags per month and budget with tax.

Using fewer rags without losing time

  • One bin per bay: rags stay within reach and don't pile up on benches.
  • The right rag in the right place: coloured for mechanics, white only for body work and painted surfaces.
  • A closed metal container for oily rags, emptied daily. See oily rags and fire risk.
  • Lightly soiled rags (water, dust) can be washed and reused. See storing, washing and disposing of rags.

Frequently asked questions

How many rags are in a 25 lb bag?

We sell by weight because piece sizes vary. That's why the method uses pounds per week rather than a number of rags.

Which rag should a garage order?

Coloured T-shirt rags at $32 per 25 lb bag for mechanics. Keep white T-shirt rags at $42 for body work. Terry is out of stock for now.
