Cleaning up an oil spill with rags

An overflowing oil change, a tipped jug, a leak under a vehicle: here's how to clean up a small oil spill cleanly and safely.

Before you start

  • Stop the source: stand the jug up, put a drain pan under the leak.
  • Remove ignition sources if it's gasoline or a solvent: no flames, no sparks, ventilate.
  • Wear oil-resistant gloves and check the product's safety data sheet (SDS).

The steps

  1. Contain: ring the spill with rags or an absorbent sock so it can't spread, especially towards a drain.
  2. Absorb from the outside in with coloured T-shirt rags. Lay them down, let them soak, lift them without scrubbing at first.
  3. Put each soaked rag straight into a closed metal container.
  4. Degrease the remaining stain with a floor degreaser, then wipe with clean rags.
  5. Dry the surface to prevent slips.

Don't

  • Never rinse oil towards a floor drain, storm sewer or ditch.
  • Don't leave soaked rags in a pile or an open trash can: see oily rags and fire risk.
  • Don't put oily rags in a washer or dryer.

After the cleanup

Rags soaked with oil, fuel or coolant don't go in the regular trash. Households take them to an ecocentre; businesses have them collected by a specialized company. See soiled rags at work.

Large spills

Rags are for small indoor spills. For a large volume, or a leak that reaches soil, a waterway or a sewer, use a proper spill kit and report it to Urgence-Environnement at Québec's Ministry of the Environment. If there's danger, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

How do I clean oil off a garage floor?

Contain the spill, absorb it with rags from the outside in, degrease the remaining stain and dry the floor. Put the soaked rags in a closed metal container.

Can I rinse oil down the drain?

No. Oil must never go into a floor drain, storm sewer or ditch. Absorb it and have the rags disposed of properly.

What do I do with oil-soaked rags?

Keep them in a closed metal container, then take them to an ecocentre (households) or have them collected by a specialized company (businesses).
