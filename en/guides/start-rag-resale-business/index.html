Business idea: resell recycled rags to garages and factories

A simple small business to start: buy your bags at distributor price from Entretien Laval, load your truck or van and sell them for cash to your customers.

Request a quoteinfo@entretienlaval.ca

Why rags

Garages, machine and welding shops, factories, farms and cleaning companies use rags every week. It's a consumable that gets bought again and again: a happy customer reorders regularly. In many regions, especially north of Montréal (Laurentides, Lanaudière, Mauricie, Abitibi, Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean), few suppliers visit small businesses in person.

Your buying price

From 50 bags of 25 lb, you pay the distributor price (before tax):

  • Colored T-shirt or terry: $28 per 25 lb bag instead of $32
  • White T-shirt or terry: $38 per 25 lb bag instead of $42

Mixed products are fine. You load your truck at 1355 boul. Le Corbusier, Laval.

How it works

  1. Email info@entretienlaval.ca with the products and number of bags (minimum 50).
  2. Pay for the order and agree on a loading time.
  3. Load your truck or van in Laval.
  4. Do your route: garages, dealerships, shops, factories, farms, cleaning companies. You set your resale price and collect payment directly.
  5. Come back to refill when your stock runs low.

Example

Example only: 50 colored bags cost $1,400 before tax. If you resell them at $40 a bag, you collect $2,000, a $600 gross margin before fuel, your time and taxes. Your resale price, customers and region are up to you; Entretien Laval does not guarantee any income.

Tips to get started

  • Start with garages and shops in your area: they are the biggest users.
  • Keep white bags for painters, cabinetmakers and finishing shops.
  • Visit on a regular schedule: customers get used to reordering.
  • Check the obligations for self-employed workers or businesses in Québec (business registration, sales taxes) with government services.

Frequently asked questions

What small business can I start with a truck in Québec?

Reselling recycled wiping rags to garages, shops and factories is a simple option: Entretien Laval, in Laval, sells to resellers at distributor price from 50 bags of 25 lb ($28 colored, $38 white, before tax). You load your truck in Laval and set your own resale price.

Do I need a warehouse to resell rags?

No, not to start. You can buy 50 bags, carry them in your truck and come back to Laval when your stock runs low.

Does Entretien Laval guarantee income to resellers?

No. We supply the rags at distributor price; your resale price, customers and results are up to you.
