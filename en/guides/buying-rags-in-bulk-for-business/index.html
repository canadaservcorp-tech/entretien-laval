Buying rags in bulk for your business

Buying rags for your own business, not to resell? Here's how to choose, how much to order, how delivery works and what you need for your books.

25 lb bag: colour $32 · white $42 · Laval delivery $15 (before tax)

Order onlineRequest a quote

1. Choose the product

  • Coloured T-shirt, $32 per 25 lb bag: oil, grease, tools, hands, everyday cleaning. Most businesses' pick.
  • White T-shirt, $42 per 25 lb bag: paint, stain, solvents, glass, stainless steel, pale surfaces.
  • Terry (coloured and white) is out of stock for now.

All rags are recycled cotton, washed in hot water, sorted and heat-treated, with no buttons or zippers, in 25 lb bags (11.3 kg). Samples on request.

ECO Rags: recycled cotton rags in 25 lb bags, coloured $32 · white $42 before tax, delivered in Laval, Montréal and both Shores. Order online · Wholesale (50 bags +)

2. Estimate the quantity

Start with a few bags, note how long they last, then use the calculator. For a garage, see how many rags per month. Plan the space: a 25 lb bag is compact, but 20 bags fill a corner of a warehouse.

3. Choose delivery or pickup

Delivered within 1 to 3 business days of payment. Outside these zones, contact us: we'll tell you what's possible for your address. See the towns we serve on the delivery page.

4. Order and pay

  • Up to 10 bags: order online, pay with PayPal or a credit or debit card.
  • More than 10 bags, or Interac e-Transfer: request a quote or email info@entretienlaval.ca.
  • Regular delivery: every 2 weeks or every month, at the same price, without calling again.

5. For your books

You get a registered business invoice with GST and QST (Entretien Laval, NEQ 2280629637). For responsible purchasing files, a supplier sheet is available on the wholesale rags page.

6. The volume price

From 50 bags, the distributor price applies: $28 coloured and $38 white per 25 lb bag, before tax, pickup only in Laval. A pallet holds 45 bags. If you resell the rags, see start a rag resale business.

Frequently asked questions

What do bulk rags cost for a business?

$32 per 25 lb bag for coloured T-shirt and $42 for white T-shirt, before tax. From 50 bags: $28 and $38, pickup only in Laval.

Do you deliver to businesses outside Greater Montreal?

Online delivery covers Laval, Montréal, the North Shore and the South Shore. Elsewhere, contact us at 438-831-3611 or info@entretienlaval.ca to find out what's possible.

Can I get a sample before a large order?

Yes, on request, to check the quality and piece size.
