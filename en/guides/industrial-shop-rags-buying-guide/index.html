Machining, mechanics, printing, maintenance: the right industrial rag depends on what you wipe. Here's how to choose and how many to plan for.

What an industrial rag must be

No metal, buttons or zippers, so nothing scratches parts or machines. Washed, sorted and cut, ready to use. Our recycled rags are hot-water washed and treated against bed bugs, one type per 25 lb bag.

What's the difference between industrial rags and shop rags?

None in practice: both are recycled cotton rags for wiping oil, grease and solvents in plants, garages and shops.

Which rag is best for a machine shop?

Colored terry: thick and absorbent for cutting oil. Our rags have no metal or buttons that could scratch parts.
