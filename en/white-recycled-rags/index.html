Two textures

White T-shirt

Thin, soft cotton jersey. To apply and wipe stain and varnish, polish, clean printing presses, stainless steel and delicate surfaces.

White terry

Premium thick towel cloth. Very absorbent, for heavy excess, spills and tool cleaning where you want no color at all.

Why white

Dyed rags can bleed with solvents, stain or moisture. White removes that risk and shows the dirt. For everyday wiping, colored rags cost less.
