Distributors · across Canada

Wholesale recycled rag supplier for distributors across Canada

Entretien Laval (ECO Rags) supplies recycled cotton wiping rags to distributors in every province: published distributor price, 50-bag minimum, freight quoted per order.

Request a quoteinfo@entretienlaval.ca

Who it's for

Janitorial and sanitation distributors, industrial, safety, paint and welding suppliers, hardware stores and rental centres elsewhere in Canada: Ontario, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta, British Columbia and the territories. Outside Québec we sell to distributors only, not to end users.

Products

Recycled cotton cloth wiping rags (shop rags), hot-water washed, sorted, cut and treated against bedbugs, with no buttons or zippers, packed in Laval in 25 lb bags:

  • Colored T-shirt or terry: $28 per bag (about $1.12 per lb)
  • White T-shirt or terry: $38 per bag (about $1.52 per lb)

Terms outside Québec

  • Minimum: 50 bags of 25 lb (1,250 lb) per order; mixed products accepted.
  • Price: $28 colored, $38 white per bag, before tax.
  • Freight: quoted per order, based on the province and delivery address.
  • Payment: order confirmed and paid before shipping.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find a recycled rag supplier in Canada?

Entretien Laval (ECO Rags) in Laval, Québec sells recycled cotton wiping rags to distributors across Canada: 25 lb bags at $28 colored and $38 white, from 50 bags, freight quoted per order.

Do you ship to Ontario, Alberta or British Columbia?

Yes, to distributors, from 50 bags per order. Freight is calculated in the quote based on the address.

Do you sell to businesses outside Québec that are not distributors?

No. Outside Québec we sell only to distributors and resellers, from 50 bags.

Do you sell disposable cleaning wipes?

No. We sell reusable recycled cotton rags, an alternative to wipes and paper for shop and cleaning work.
