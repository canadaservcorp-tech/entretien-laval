How to estimate your usage

The simplest way: order a few 25 lb bags and note how long they last. Then divide 25 lb by the number of weeks to get your pounds per week.

Outside Laval and Montréal

The minimum is 1,000 lb per delivery (40 bags, mixed products welcome). The calculator shows how many months of usage that represents. See the delivery terms.
