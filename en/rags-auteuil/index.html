Delivery in Auteuil

Auteuil, in northern Laval, has car detailing shops, car washes and neighbourhood garages. What they need: plenty of rags for wheels, engines and door jambs, and clean rags that don't streak glass.

Delivery in Auteuil: $15 per order, from 1 bag, within 1 to 3 business days of payment. Auteuil is in our Laval zone (H7 postal codes): you can order online and pay with PayPal or card.

For car detailing and car washes

  • Wheels, engines, door jambs: coloured T-shirt rags: get them dirty and toss them, without wearing out your microfibers. See car detailing and car wash.
  • Glass and chrome: white T-shirt rags, low-lint. See rags for glass and stainless steel.
  • Paintwork: keep your microfibers for paint; recycled cotton handles the rest. See lint-free rags.

Example order

An Auteuil detailing shop taking 2 coloured bags for wheels and engines and 2 white bags for glass pays 2 × $32 + 2 × $42 + $15 delivery = $163 before tax. With free pickup in Laval: $148 before tax. Example calculation only; GST and QST extra.

Frequently asked questions

How much is delivery in Auteuil?

$15 per order, from 1 bag, before tax. Delivered within 1 to 3 business days of payment, or free pickup at 1355 boul. Le Corbusier, Laval.

Do the rags replace microfiber?

Not on paint. They replace microfiber for wheels, engines, door jambs and big messes, where it wears out fast.

Which rag for car windows?

White T-shirt rags, clean and dry for the final wipe. Skip fabric softener when you wash them: it streaks.
