How it works

  1. Choose the products and the number of 25 lb bags.
  2. Enter your contact details and your Laval or Montréal address (postal code starting with H).
  3. Pay with PayPal, or with a credit or debit card without a PayPal account.
  4. We contact you to set the delivery date.

Delivery

Laval (postal code H7): $15 per order, from 1 bag. Montréal (other H postal codes): $25 per order, minimum 2 bags. Plus tax.

Prices and taxes

Prices are shown before tax. GST (5%) and QST (9.975%) are calculated on the products and delivery and shown before you pay. The price is the same whatever the payment method. You get a registered business invoice.

Other regions, large orders, Interac e-Transfer

Outside Laval and Montréal, for more than 10 25 lb bags, or to pay by Interac e-Transfer, request a quote or email info@entretienlaval.ca.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a PayPal account?

No. The card button lets you pay with a credit or debit card without a PayPal account.

When is it delivered?

We contact you after payment to set the delivery date at your Laval or Montréal address.

How much is delivery?

Laval (postal code H7): $15 per order, from 1 bag. Montréal (other H postal codes): $25 per order, minimum 2 bags. Plus tax.

Is there a fee for paying online?

No. You pay the product price, delivery and taxes, the same as an order by quote.
