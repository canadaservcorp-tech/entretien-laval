Job sites use a lot of them

Wiping tools, glue and sealant, cleaning a plumbing joint, protecting a surface, catching a spill: a job site goes through bags of rags fast. Colored rags are the budget choice.

  • Colored T-shirt: caulking, glue, tools, hands.
  • Colored terry: oil, grease, big spills, heavy equipment.
  • White: finishing, painting and visible surfaces.

Who it's for

General and specialty contractors, plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, roofers, flooring installers and post-construction cleaning crews.

Delivery to the site

We deliver to any address you choose: office, warehouse or job site. Outside Laval and Montréal, 1,000 lb minimum per delivery. See the terms or request a quote.

On the job site

Keep a bag in each trailer or truck. Use colored rags for tools, equipment and cleanup, and white rags for painting and finishing trades. Rags soaked with solvent or stain go in a closed metal container.

Contractors with several sites can order in volume: see wholesale.
