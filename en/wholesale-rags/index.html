Reliable supply

Janitorial supply distributors, hardware stores, industrial suppliers, rental centres and large companies: we supply sorted recycled wiping rags, packed in 25 lb bags and ready to resell or use.

  • 4 products: T-shirt or terry, white or colored.
  • Unlimited quantity, deliveries scheduled to your needs.
  • No metal, buttons or zippers: nothing to scratch stainless steel, paint or machines.
  • Washed in hot water, sorted and treated against bedbugs, packed in Laval.
  • Cut into ready-to-use pieces: no time lost.
  • Clean, with a fresh smell.

Terms

Price per 25 lb bag: $32 colored, $42 white, before tax. Outside Laval and Montréal, 1,000 lb minimum (40 bags) per delivery, delivery fee based on your location. Orders confirmed and paid one week before delivery.

Volume orders

Quantity is unlimited. A delivery outside Laval and Montréal starts at 1,000 lb, or 40 25 lb bags, and you can mix all four products in one order. For recurring or large-volume orders, request a quote: we price the order and delivery based on your address and frequency.

For distributors and resellers

Janitorial distributors, hardware stores, garage and shop suppliers: our rags come in sealed 25 lb bags, easy to store and resell. Include your expected volumes and delivery addresses in your request.

How an order works

  1. You request a quote with the quantity and address.
  2. We confirm the price, delivery fee and date.
  3. The order is confirmed and paid one week before delivery.
  4. We deliver to your address, with an invoice showing GST and QST.
