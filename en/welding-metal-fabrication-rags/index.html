Where our rags are used in a metal shop

  • Before welding: wiping oil, grease and dirt off the steel, with your usual solvent.
  • After grinding and drilling: removing dust and cutting oil from parts.
  • Before painting or coating: the final wipe of the surfaces.
  • Maintenance: welding stations, saws, press brakes, drills, hands and benches.

Which rag to choose

UseRecommended rag
Oil, grease, everyday cleaningColored (T-shirt or terry), $32
Degreasing before welding or painting, finished partsWhite T-shirt, $42: no dye transfer
Oil or coolant spillsColored terry, $32

Our rags have no buttons, zippers or metal parts, so they don't scratch parts.

Safety near sparks

Keep rags, clean or dirty, away from the welding and grinding area. Rags soaked with solvent or oil go in a closed metal container. See the guide oily rags and fire risk.
