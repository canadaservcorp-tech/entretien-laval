What truck garages use our rags for

  • Oil changes and greasing: engines, differentials, fifth wheels and grease points.
  • Diesel and fluids: small spills, caps and fittings.
  • Inspection and maintenance: wiping parts before checking them.
  • Trailers and equipment: brakes, axles and mechanisms.

Which product to choose

Colored terry ($32 per 25 lb bag) holds the most oil and grease. Colored T-shirt works for everyday cleanup. A fleet goes through many rags: see our bulk order terms.

Safety

Oil-soaked rags belong in a closed metal container. See the guide oily rags and fire risk.
