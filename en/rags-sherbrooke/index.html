Disposing of dirty rags

Rags soaked with oil, solvent or paint are hazardous waste. Households take them to the ecocentres or household hazardous waste collections run by the City of Sherbrooke; businesses use a specialized waste pickup company. Check local rules. See also the guide storing, washing and disposing of rags.
