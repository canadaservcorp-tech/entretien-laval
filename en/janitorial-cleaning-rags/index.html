Rags for cleaning crews

For dusting, spills, floors, mechanical rooms and post-renovation cleanup, a cotton rag absorbs more than paper and lasts longer. It also cuts down the bags of paper going in the trash.

  • Colored T-shirt: general use, dust, surfaces and equipment.
  • Terry: floors, big spills, mechanical rooms, building garages.
  • White: where you want to see the dirt and avoid any color transfer.

Clean and safe for your clients

Every lot is washed and treated against bedbugs before sorting and packing in Laval, so you can bring them into your clients' buildings with confidence.

Frequently asked questions

Are the rags good for windows?

For glass and stainless steel, thin, low-lint white T-shirt rags are the best choice among our products.

Can I use them with cleaning products?

Yes, cotton handles common cleaners. Always follow the product manufacturer's instructions.
